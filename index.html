⏰ Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Can you book more than one hour?

Yes. Choose one, two, or three hours on the booking form.

Which times can you book?

Morning slots run from 7:00 am to 9:00 am. Evening slots run from 5:00 pm to 10:00 pm.

How do you see your booking total?

Submit a valid booking, and we’ll show your total.

Can you split the court cost?

Yes. Enter your court price, hours, and players on the Split cost page.
